# ADR 0006: Keep complete projects in the working flow

Status: accepted, 2026-09-12.

## Problem

The twelve original starters were hidden behind a small header control. Half were one- or two-part mechanism studies. A visitor arriving at the workbench could reasonably conclude that Plega had lost its predefined projects or that the product only supported a small toy example.

## Decision

Retain every original project and add twelve authored 8–12 part compositions. Each composition uses supported parallel-step and V-fold families, individual colors and cutwork, and a distinct ordered mechanical narrative. The new projects are generated from explicit, deterministic authoring recipes into the reviewable source catalog. The independent catalog checker rejects a design with an unsafe page boundary, closed width, incompatible V domain, or overlapping motion lane. The checked-in generated TypeScript and catalog artifact carry integrity hashes.

Show a prominent project entry at the top of both the intro and workbench rail. The gallery first shows the twelve signature projects and provides All and Quick study groups, preserving the original teaching examples. The preview renders the actual source project's part order, family and size, rather than generic decorative bars. Loading a project remains an undoable edit to the same workspace and drives the live object, motion and cut plan.

The A4 and Letter single-sheet export remains strict: it rejects an oversized continuous piece instead of shrinking it. The gallery workbench chooses the existing registered A4 transfer-tile mode for larger compositions and exposes every resulting sheet in the cut-plan navigator. The full-size dimensions and tile registration marks remain in the export.

## Limits and verification

The restricted software geometry and flat cut plan are checked. No physical assembly or material durability claim is made. Acceptance requires loading a twelve-part project, reaching every part, finding a passing diagnostic, undoing the load, and viewing its connected cut plan. All 24 catalog entries must pass the independent source validation.
