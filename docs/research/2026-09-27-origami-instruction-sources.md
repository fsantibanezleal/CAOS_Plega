# Origami instruction source audit (2026-09-27)

## Decision question

Can PLEGA publish a large, free, browsable collection of downloadable origami plans and animate their steps without copying creators' protected work?

## Sources checked

| Source | What it provides | Rights / integration decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OrigamiUSA copyright policy](https://origamiusa.org/copyright) and [free diagrams](https://origamiusa.org/diagrams/free) | Traditional designs and creator-submitted downloadable diagrams | Free personal downloads are **not** permission to redistribute diagrams or animate a creator's design. Link out only unless the particular rights holder grants permission. |

## Product consequences

PLEGA distinguishes **guided** models with authored, inspectable step sequences from **illustrated** whole-sheet diagrams with explicit media licenses and **external** free-to-access plans that remain on their creators' sites. Counts are shown separately. “Thousands of guided models” may not be claimed by inflating categories or variants. An external plan is not labelled as playable or locally downloadable. Filters, searches and category counts operate on the real index. Source attribution is visible on each plan.

The bundled Tavin set is Bird base, Claws, Crane, Cup, Fish base, Flapping bird, Fox, Mouse, Pig, Rabbit, Christmas star, Jumping frog, Steamer, Waterbomb base and Sailboat. The Fox PDF states CC BY 3.0; the other fourteen show CC BY-SA 3.0 notices, including visible notices on four PDFs whose license text does not extract cleanly. Each source file is a one-page illustrated sequence, not a machine-readable animation. The diagrams retain Tavin's credit and license marks. PLEGA adds a rendered PNG preview; the unmodified PDF is available for download. The PDF hash is hard-pinned so an upstream replacement cannot silently inherit this rights decision. `python data-pipeline/import_tavin_diagrams.py verify` checks the local PDFs, previews and manifest.

The external index is a discovery layer, not a proof that every historic source link remains live. The Origami Resource Center import excludes archive snapshots and generic link labels. On 2026-09-27, `check_origami_plan_links.py` checked HTTP response headers for all 321 candidate direct file links: 286 returned a successful PDF/image response without a signed redirect and enter the displayed catalog; 35 failed, resolved to HTML, or redirected through expiring CDN token URLs and are withheld. No signed token is stored in the public snapshot. A deterministic random sample of 30 Origami Club pages returned 30 successful responses. The additional creator-hosted index found 796 public tutorial links: 400 Paper Kawaii, 160 Origami Way, 132 Origami.me pages with numbered fold steps, and 104 Origami Plus pages. Together with 924 Origami Club pages, the 286 checked file links, 32 licensed local diagrams and four original animated lessons, the UI contains 2,042 source entries. This is **not** a claim of 2,042 unique designs, local downloads, or animations. These are point-in-time availability checks, not a guarantee that links will keep working. The UI identifies external entries as links and does not offer a local download. An animation is not promised unless it is available at source.

Step animation needs an authored sequence with a geometry/state transition for every step. A slideshow of a third-party diagram or a generic crease tween is not represented as a verified folding motion. Original simplified educational animations must be labelled as such and checked against physical folding before release.
