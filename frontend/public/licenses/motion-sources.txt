PLEGA adaptations: final action histories are replayed with a pinned geometric
simulator; only geometric actions, parameters and states are retained. Data is
converted to millimetres, triangulated and compiled into continuous rigid and
hinge operations. PLEGA adds English/Spanish teaching text, diagrams, previews,
checks and a source-to-operation map. Source URL and SHA-256 are retained for
each model. No source photographs, videos or inference prose are included.
