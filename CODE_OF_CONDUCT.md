# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss evidence and ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, discriminatory conduct, threats, deliberate publication of private information and repeated disruptive behavior are unacceptable.

Keep reports and examples focused on the project. Obtain consent before sharing someone else's project files or identifying information. Explain disagreements clearly and allow maintainers to resolve scope and review decisions.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation when needed to protect the community. Report a conduct concern through an available private repository contact; if none is available, request a private channel without publishing the sensitive details. Enforcement applies to this repository and its official project discussions.
